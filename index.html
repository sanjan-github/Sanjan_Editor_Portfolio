Editing Philosophy

The goal is not noise. The goal is impact.

Every cut should push the energy forward, make the music feel tighter, and give the frame more weight.

Rhythm first

Edits are built around pacing, drops, and the feeling of movement inside the music.

Control the frame

Transitions, contrast, and motion should elevate the footage instead of covering it up.

Keep it sharp

Short-form work needs clean choices, clear focus, and a finish that still feels intentional.
